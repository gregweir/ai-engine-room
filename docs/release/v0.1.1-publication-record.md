## Post-publication verification

After publication, all three assets were downloaded into a fresh disposable
directory. Their complete filenames, byte sizes, and SHA-256 values matched the
accepted candidates and tracked checksum manifest. GitHub reported the release
as non-draft and non-prerelease, and the public tag resolved directly to the
accepted source commit above. The disposable verification downloads were then
removed.

The packages remain unsigned. Matching checksums establish byte identity; they
do not provide a cryptographic publisher signature. The safe-install guidance
in the [user guide](../user-guide.md#before-you-install) remains authoritative.
